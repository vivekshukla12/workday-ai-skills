# Source basis

Use the most current accessible versions and record exact title, publication or access date, relevant section, and URL or controlled location in a private source note linked from the relevant Evidence Reference, Basis or run snapshot. The simplified workbook has no Sources sheet. Keep source titles, dates and public URLs where applicable; never publish private evidence.

## Workday documentation

- Workday Human Capital Management documentation: worker, organization, position, job, skills, recruiting, compensation, absence, talent, security, and effective-dated concepts.
- Workday Reporting and Analytics documentation: secured custom reporting, calculated fields, analytics, and governed distribution.
- Workday People Analytics documentation: workforce insights, topics, and people-analysis context.
- Workday Adaptive Planning documentation: workforce and financial planning, scenario versions, forecasts, and plan-versus-actual analysis.
- Workday Financial Management documentation: companies, ledgers, currencies, worktags or dimensions, accounting, statistics, and allocations.
- Workday Payroll, Time Tracking, Absence, Recruiting, Skills, and Prism documentation when those products are in scope.

Workday documentation describes product capabilities. Verify tenant configuration, security, data availability, calculated-field logic, report filters, and effective dating before designing an extract.

## External guidance

- ISO 22301, *Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements*: continuity governance and lifecycle. Use an appropriately licensed copy for detailed requirements. https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html
- International Labour Organization, *Business continuity planning guidelines for small and medium-sized enterprises* (2009): business priorities, dependencies, risk impact, and response planning. https://www.ilo.org/publications/business-continuity-planning-guidelines-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises
- Relevant public authorities for health, weather, civil protection, sanctions, cyber threats, employment, privacy, and sector regulation.
- Company-controlled business continuity, risk, finance, accounting, employment, collective-agreement, privacy, retention, information-security, and delegated-authority policies.

Prefer primary and authoritative sources. Treat generic white papers as context, not as proof of company-specific risk, probability, or compliance.
