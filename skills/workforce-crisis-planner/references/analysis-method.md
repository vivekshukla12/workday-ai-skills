# Workforce and financial gap analysis

## Baseline

Declare the analytical grain and time period before calculating. Prefer period by legal entity, service, location, and role or skill. Record baseline source versions and reconciliation controls.

## Core measures

- `Available capacity = scheduled FTE or hours - absence - vacancy - training or restrictions - scenario loss`
- `Required capacity = minimum service demand / verified productivity`, or an approved role-coverage requirement.
- `Capacity gap = available capacity - required capacity`; show units and percentage of required capacity.
- `Critical-role coverage = available qualified workers / minimum qualified workers`.
- `Skill coverage = qualified, currently valid supply / scenario-adjusted skill demand`.
- `Location concentration = critical role or service capacity in one dependency area / total relevant capacity`.
- `Replacement lead-time gap = feasible redeployment, training, recruitment, clearance, or supplier lead time - scenario onset time`.
- `Labor-cost exposure = baseline workforce cost + scenario overtime + premiums + contractors + relocation + severance + retention cost`.
- `Revenue or margin exposure = at-risk service volume x contribution per unit`; keep penalties separate.
- `Cash survival horizon = available liquidity / scenario-adjusted net cash outflow`; return not available when the denominator is missing or nonpositive.

Do not apply a universal severity score unless the user approves factors, weights, and thresholds. Present the components: business criticality, magnitude, onset speed, duration, reversibility, people impact, legal constraints, and evidence confidence.

## Analysis sequence

1. Reconcile source populations and monetary controls.
2. Establish normal capacity, demand, cost, absence, vacancy, attrition, and seasonality.
3. Apply scenario assumptions as a separate version.
4. Calculate capacity, role, skill, location, replacement-time, cost, revenue, margin, and cash effects.
5. Test service minimums and linked customer, supplier, facility, technology, and regulatory dependencies.
6. Identify shortages, single points of failure, excess capacity, unaffordable cost, liquidity constraints, control failures, and missing evidence.
7. Run sensitivity ranges for material uncertain assumptions and combined scenarios.
8. Record every gap with stable ID, cause, evidence, owner, status, and confidence.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Keep risk severity and data confidence separate.
- Distinguish zero from missing and not applicable.
- Do not interpret correlation as causation.
- Check aggregate findings for masking across sites, roles, contracts, or protected groups, while maintaining lawful access.
- Describe what the data supports, what is inferred, and what remains unknown.
- Validate recommendations with HR, Finance, Operations, Risk, Legal, Privacy, and employee-representation stakeholders as applicable.
